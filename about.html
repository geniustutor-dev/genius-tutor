About Us

Genius Tutor CBT

Genius Tutor helps students get used to the real computer-based test (CBT) setting before the day of the exam, so they walk into the exam hall already used to the screen.

What you can do

  • Full timed exams that follow the JAMB UTME style.
  • Practice sessions at real exam speed in the subjects you choose.
  • WAEC, NECO and GCE style papers with a grade after you submit.
  • A question palette like the real exam hall, with flagging and jumping between questions.
  • Instant marking and a full answer review with explanations after every attempt.

Important

Genius Tutor is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to JAMB, WAEC, NECO or any government body. The questions are past practice questions provided for revision only.
